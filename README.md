# COL Academic Expo: Sleep Quality Research

This repository hosts the public website for a COL Academic Expo quantitative
research project about sleep quality among high school students.

Live site:

https://cnlovemiku.github.io/COL-Academic-Expo/

## Project Overview

The website presents a student-led research experience investigating how stress,
depressive symptoms, and electronic device use relate to sleep quality. It is
designed for an Expo booth setting, where visitors should be able to understand
the research question, evidence, learning process, and takeaway within a short
visit.

Research question:

How do stress, depressive symptoms, and electronic device use relate to an
individual's sleep quality among high school students?

## Key Findings Presented

- 44 survey participants were included in the descriptive analysis.
- 89% of participants reported falling asleep between 1-2 a.m.
- 86% reported using electronic devices for 1-2 hours before sleep.
- 59% identified self-expectation as a major source of stress.

These findings are presented as descriptive patterns, not causal proof.

## Website Sections
